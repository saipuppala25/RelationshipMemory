TIPS
----
- Supported photo formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp
  (if you use a different format, update the src="" in gallery.html)
- Supported video formats: .mp4 (H.264 recommended for best browser support)
- For best results, resize photos to around 1200px wide before adding them.
  Large files will slow down the page.
- You can add more photos by duplicating a gallery-item block in gallery.html
  and updating the src and alt attributes.
